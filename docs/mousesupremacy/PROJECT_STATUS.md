## Findings and direction

Reuse upstream ROM fingerprinting, battle reads, frame-driven inputs, render geometry, companion layouts, field snapshots and capture. Before enabling a capability, prove the actual host event, displayed transform, sole input owner and ROM readiness. Movement additionally needs legal walkable edges and active entity contact rules. [Source audit](UPSTREAM_AUDIT.md) · [Architecture](ARCHITECTURE.md)

Headquarters retained Android-first/WSA with a Windows-hosted Android fallback, exact English FireRed rev 1 first, native UI/selection, read-only observation, ordinary emulator keys, exact-commit on-demand APKs and disposable signing for isolated experiments. Native Windows parity and retained distribution have separate approval gates. [Decision and risk register](DECISIONS_AND_RISKS.md) · [Requirements and capability index](REQUIREMENTS.md)
